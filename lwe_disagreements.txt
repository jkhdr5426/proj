INTERPRETATION
====================================================================

A 'rugged' free energy landscape means the Metropolis-Hastings
sampler found multiple competing local minima when trying to
navigate from a random guess toward the true secret.

BKZ models a single-pathway lattice reduction. It does not
account for metastability or landscape roughness. If umbrella
sampling finds a rugged landscape in a region where BKZ also
struggles (or fails entirely), this suggests the hardness of
those instances comes from a different mechanism than BKZ assumes.

STRONG GAP configs are the most interesting: BKZ cannot even
find an attack path, yet the umbrella sampler sees structural
complexity (rugosity > 0) rather than a flat cliff. This
means the search space is not uniformly hard — it has texture
that neither BKZ nor brute force can exploit cleanly.

This is the novel contribution: umbrella sampling reveals
hardness texture that BKZ security models miss entirely.
